\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Transportation inference from sparse observations.}
Developed a latent-dynamics framework for network-wide
travel-time inference from sparse and reporting-delayed
trip records, combining low-rank representations with
adaptive filtering to update estimates as new
observations become available.

\item \textbf{Probabilistic modeling of ride-hailing demand.}
Studied sparse demand patterns and dependencies across
ride-sourcing services through zero-inflated count models
and joint noncrossing-quantile prediction, with an
emphasis on characterizing predictive uncertainty.

\item \textbf{Decision-focused fleet rebalancing.}
Developed a learning framework that distinguishes
predicting passenger demand from determining where
vehicles should be repositioned. Regional coverage
targets are learned from downstream service and
vehicle-reuse consequences and implemented through
network-flow optimization.

\end{itemize}
